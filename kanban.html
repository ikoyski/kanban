A Dedication to My Family

Behind every project is a story, and behind this project are the two people who inspire me the most.

I dedicate this web application to my wonderful wife, whose love, support, patience, and understanding have given me the encouragement to keep learning, creating, and pursuing the things I enjoy. Her constant support means more to me than words can express, and I am truly grateful to have her by my side through every idea, challenge, and accomplishment.

I also dedicate this to my son, who inspires me to keep growing and becoming a better person. Watching him grow from a little boy into a young man is one of the greatest joys of my life. My hope is that he grows up to be a good man—kind, respectful, humble, responsible, and strong enough to face life's challenges with courage and faith. May he always remember to follow his dreams, learn from his mistakes, treat others with kindness, and never forget the values that matter most.

Everything I create carries a little piece of the inspiration I receive from them.

To my wife and my son: thank you for being my inspiration, my motivation, and one of the greatest blessings in my life. This work is dedicated to both of you, with all my love.

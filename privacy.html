Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

This policy explains what Kanban collects, why, and the choices you have. It is written to match how the app works today.

Who runs Kanban

Kanban is a personal project run by ikoyski in the Philippines. In this policy, “I” and “me” mean the person running it. You can reach me at the email address in the Contact section.

What I collect

  • Account details: your display name, email address and password when you sign up. You can add an avatar.
  • Your content: the boards, columns, cards, labels, due dates, comments and board members you create or are added to.
  • Technical data: your IP address and basic request details, which are processed to deliver the service, apply rate limits and block abuse.

What is stored in your browser

Kanban saves a few items in your browser’s local storage so the app works: your login token, your display name and avatar, your theme choice, and the board you opened last. These stay on your device until you sign out or clear your browser data. I don’t use advertising or analytics cookies. Cloudflare Turnstile, the bot check on sign-up and sign-in, may set its own cookies.

How I use it

  • To run your account and show your boards to you and the people you share them with.
  • To send emails you ask for, such as password reset links.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

I don’t sell your data and I don’t use it for advertising.

Who else handles your data

These services process some data so that Kanban works:

  • Cloudflare provides the CDN, firewall, network tunnel and the Turnstile bot check. Requests to Kanban pass through it.
  • Resend delivers emails such as password resets, so it receives your email address and the message.
  • ui-avatars.com generates the default avatar image. Your display name is sent to it in the image address.
  • jsDelivr, the Tailwind CSS CDN and Google Fonts serve scripts, styles and fonts to your browser, so they can see your IP address.

Your boards and account data are stored in a PostgreSQL database on infrastructure I operate.

Who can see your content

People you share a board with can see that board’s content, including your display name, avatar and comments. Boards you don’t share are visible only to you and to me as the operator when maintenance requires it.

How long I keep it

I keep your data while your account exists. You can delete boards and cards yourself, and you can export a board as a JSON file from the app. To delete your whole account and its data, email me and I’ll remove it.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, and similar laws where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and object to how it is used. Email me and I’ll respond. You can also complain to the Philippine National Privacy Commission.

Security

Traffic to Kanban is encrypted with HTTPS. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password. If a breach affects your data, I’ll notify you as the law requires.

Children

Kanban isn’t intended for children. If you believe a child has created an account, email me and I’ll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy in a way that matters, I’ll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions or requests: admin@ikoyski.top.

Who runs Kanban

Kanban is a personal project operated by ikoyski in the Philippines. In this policy, “I” and “me” refer to the operator. You can reach me using the details in the Contact section.

Information I collect

  • Account and identity information: when you sign in with Google or GitHub through the Kanban authentication service, it receives identity information such as your provider's stable account identifier, verified email address, display name and profile image URL. Kanban does not receive your provider password.
  • Your content: boards, columns, cards, labels, due dates, comments and board memberships that you create or that others share with you.
  • Technical and security information: IP address, request metadata and authentication or service logs used to operate the service, enforce rate limits, troubleshoot issues and help prevent abuse.

Information stored in your browser

The Kanban web application stores an OAuth access token and related expiry information in browser local storage under its application storage key. It also uses browser storage for account display information and preferences such as the selected theme and last opened board. During sign-in, a temporary PKCE verifier and OAuth state are kept in session storage to protect the authorization redirect flow. These values are stored in your browser; signing out clears the application's authentication data. Clearing browser storage may also remove saved preferences. Anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to access information stored there, so protect your device and browser session.

The public website and application load some third-party scripts, styles, libraries and fonts. Those providers may receive your IP address and standard browser request information when your browser requests those resources. I do not use advertising or analytics cookies as part of the application described here.

How I use information

  • To authenticate you and operate your account.
  • To store, display and synchronize boards and content with people you have authorized to access them.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the service.
  • To respond to support, privacy and account-deletion requests.

I do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Service providers and third parties

Kanban relies on service providers and external resources to operate. These may include:

  • Google and GitHub: provide federated sign-in services. Each provider processes sign-in activity under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: provides network, DNS, CDN, firewall and related infrastructure services through which some requests may pass.
  • Google Fonts, the Tailwind CSS CDN and jsDelivr: deliver fonts, styles and frontend libraries to your browser and may receive standard connection information such as your IP address.
  • Hosting and database providers: process or store application data and service infrastructure on my behalf. Kanban account and board data is stored in a PostgreSQL database.

The service providers and infrastructure used may change as the project evolves. I will update this policy when a material change affects how information is handled.

AI assistants and MCP integrations

Kanban supports compatible AI assistants through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), including Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude and OpenAI ChatGPT where available. You must authorize an assistant before it can access your Kanban account through this integration.

When you use an assistant with Kanban, information in your prompts and the Kanban content needed to answer your request may be sent to and processed by the AI provider. Depending on the tools available and your instructions, the assistant may retrieve boards, columns, cards or comments, or create and modify content such as cards, column positions and comments. Actions are authenticated using your access token and are subject to the permissions of your Kanban account.

The MCP service validates the access token and forwards authorized requests to the Kanban API. The MCP service is designed to be stateless and does not maintain a separate persistent copy of your Kanban content or access tokens. The AI provider may process prompts, responses and information you choose to share according to its own terms, privacy policy, retention settings and data controls. Review the relevant provider policy before connecting an assistant or sharing sensitive information.

Who can see your content

People you share a board with can access that board's content according to their assigned roles, including information such as your display name, profile image and comments. Content on boards you have not shared is not made available to other users through normal board-sharing features. As the operator, I may access information when reasonably necessary to maintain, secure or support the service.

Retention and account deletion

I retain account information and content while your account remains active or as needed to operate, secure and maintain the service. You can delete boards and cards yourself and export a board as a JSON file from the app.

Account deletion is currently handled manually; there is no self-service account deletion feature yet. To request deletion, email admin@ikoyski.top. I will review and process the request manually. Some information may remain for a limited period in backups, logs or records where retention is necessary for security, operational or legal reasons; such information will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, you may request access to or correction, portability or deletion of your personal information, or exercise other rights available to you. Send requests to the Contact address below. You may also raise a concern with the Philippine National Privacy Commission.

Security

Traffic to Kanban is protected using HTTPS. I use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your Google or GitHub account, devices and browser profile secure. If a personal data breach requires notification under applicable law, I will provide notice as required.

Children

Kanban isn’t intended for children. If you believe a child has created an account, email me and I’ll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy in a way that matters, I’ll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions or requests: admin@ikoyski.top.

Questions and answers

What is a kanban board?
A kanban board shows your work as cards in columns such as To do, In progress and Done. You move a card across as the work moves, so it is easy to see what is waiting, what is underway and what is finished.
Can I share a board with my team?
Yes. Invite people by email as Owners, Editors or Viewers. Owners and Editors can change the board; Viewers can read it but not edit it.
What can I write on a card?
Descriptions and comments are rich text, with bold, lists, links and code blocks. Cards also take priorities, due dates and labels.
Is Kanban free and open source?
Yes. Kanban is open source under the GPL-3.0 license, and the code is on GitHub.
Can I manage Kanban with an AI assistant?
Yes. Supported assistants such as Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude and OpenAI ChatGPT can connect through MCP where available. After you authorize an assistant, it can use supported board and card actions within your account permissions. The AI provider processes your prompts and the information you choose to share with it under its own policies.
What information is shared with an AI assistant?
Only connect an assistant when you are comfortable sharing the information needed for your request. Prompts and relevant Kanban content may be processed by the AI provider under its own terms and privacy policy. You can review the Privacy Policy for more detail.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. The board works in the browser on phones, tablets and desktops, in light or dark mode.

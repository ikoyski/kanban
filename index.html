Questions and answers

What is a kanban board?
A kanban board shows your work as cards in columns such as To do, In progress and Done. You move a card across as the work moves, so it is easy to see what is waiting, what is underway and what is finished.
Can I share a board with my team?
Yes. Invite people by email as Owners, Editors or Viewers. Owners and Editors can change the board; Viewers can read it but not edit it.
What can I write on a card?
Descriptions and comments are rich text, with bold, lists, links and code blocks. Cards also take priorities, due dates and labels.
Is Kanban free and open source?
Yes. Kanban is open source under the GPL-3.0 license, and the code is on GitHub.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. The board works in the browser on phones, tablets and desktops, in light or dark mode.
